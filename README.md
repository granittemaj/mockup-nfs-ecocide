# Not For Sale — Graphics section, review mockup

A mockup of `/graphics/`, a section for visual investigations kept separate
from `/news/`.

- **Section index** — repository root
- **The one published piece** — `first-they-take-the-person/`

Both sit inside the live Not For Sale site chrome and use the site's own
typography and colour.

## Why a separate section

News reports what happened. Graphics takes a claim made on the frontline and
shows the working: the map, the figures, the photography and the sources, on
one page. These are built rather than written, they carry interaction, and
they are meant to stay useful after the news cycle that prompted them.

## What is real here

One investigation is published. The other eight cards are marked **In
production** or **Planned** and are shown so the section can be reviewed at
full size. Their titles are drawn from Not For Sale's actual reporting and
project areas; card imagery is placeholder, taken from existing field
photography.

## Known gaps, before this could publish

- Photography is lifted from the social carousel and machine-cropped above or
  below the burned-in captions. Framing is provisional and resolution is capped
  at 740px on the long edge. Production needs the original plates.
- Gloss copy in the investigation demonstrates the format and needs editorial
  sign-off.
- The globe's per-commodity layer carries only entries checked line by line
  against the ILAB database, so most countries currently show no goods.
- Every figure carries its source, but each still needs a source URL and an
  access date.
