Why this is not in News

News reports what happened. Graphics takes something we have argued on the frontline and shows the working: the map, the figures, the photography and the sources, assembled on one page so a reader can check it.

Built, not written

Each piece is made rather than filed. They take weeks, they carry interaction, and they are designed to stay useful long after the news cycle that prompted them.

Sourced on the page

Every figure names its source and its date. Nothing is modelled or inferred. Where evidence is missing, the page says so rather than filling the gap.
